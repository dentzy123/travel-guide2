# Weather Tips App

A practical web application and guide for getting essential weather safety tips and recommendations based on current weather conditions.

## 🌤️ Overview

This project provides comprehensive weather tips to help you stay safe and prepared for any weather condition. Whether it's sunny, rainy, snowy, cloudy, or windy - we've got you covered with practical, actionable advice.

## 📂 Project Contents

### 1. Interactive Web App (`index.html`)
A beautiful, responsive web application featuring:
- **5 Weather Conditions**: Sunny, Rainy, Cloudy, Snowy, and Windy
- **8 Tips Per Condition**: Practical advice for health, safety, and preparation
- **Interactive Dropdown**: Easy selection of weather types
- **Modern Design**: Gradient background with smooth animations
- **Mobile Responsive**: Works perfectly on all devices

### 2. Comprehensive Guide (`travel-guide/WEATHER_TIPS_GUIDE.md`)
An in-depth guide covering:
- **Detailed Safety Tips**: For each weather condition
- **Health Recommendations**: UV protection, hydration, cold weather care
- **Driving Safety**: Speed, visibility, and vehicle maintenance tips
- **Home Maintenance**: Seasonal preparations and emergency readiness
- **Community Care**: Helping neighbors during severe weather
- **Quick Reference Chart**: At-a-glance weather safety tips

## 🌈 Weather Tips Overview

### ☀️ Sunny Weather
- Apply sunscreen (SPF 30+) every 2 hours
- Wear sunglasses and a hat
- Stay hydrated throughout the day
- Wear light-colored, breathable clothing
- Limit sun exposure during peak hours (10 AM - 4 PM)

### 🌧️ Rainy Weather
- Use an umbrella or waterproof jacket
- Wear non-slip shoes
- Reduce driving speed and increase following distance
- Use headlights for better visibility
- Check windshield wipers regularly

### ☁️ Cloudy Weather
- UV rays still penetrate clouds - use sunscreen
- Use headlights during daytime
- Check forecasts for weather changes
- Wear layers as temperatures may vary
- Perfect for outdoor activities without intense heat

### ❄️ Snowy Weather
- Wear insulated, waterproof boots
- Layer your clothing properly
- Protect exposed skin from frostbite
- Drive slowly with appropriate tires
- Check on elderly neighbors

### 💨 Windy Weather
- Secure loose outdoor items
- Avoid standing near tall trees
- Hold onto railings to prevent losing balance
- Reduce driving speed, especially large vehicles
- Use moisturizer to prevent wind-chafed skin
